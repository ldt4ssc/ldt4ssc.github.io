# Strategic priorities

Five priorities translate the strategy into action. Each carries concrete recommendations for
three audiences — **pilots**, the **LDT4SSC consortium**, and **other stakeholders** across
local communities and EU initiatives, including policymakers.

Use the tabs under each priority to see what applies to you.

## Capabilities that foster sustainability

Sustainability is **not a fixed stage of maturity** but a dynamic set of conditions that must be
sufficiently in place for LDTs to function, generate value and keep evolving.

The strategy maps **18 capabilities** across six themes. To stay practical, these are mapped
against the requirements (Rq) and recommendations (Rc) already defined in the open-call texts,
so they ask nothing new of pilots beyond what the calls specify.

## 1 · Coordinating towards interoperability and multi-stakeholder collaboration

The foundation of a scalable European LDT ecosystem is the ability to **connect heterogeneous
systems** across communities, sectors and borders. Interoperability frameworks and tools already
exist, but their consistent adoption and operationalisation remain limited — calling for strong
leadership and coordination across initiatives.

=== "For pilots"

    1. Establish **clear governance and strategic leadership** for LDT development and long-term
       operation beyond pilot implementation, and describe it in your deliverables.
    2. Stay proactive in identifying **opportunities for replication and collaboration** through
       LDT4SSC-wide activities and beyond.

=== "For LDT4SSC"

    1. Provide practical guidance and tools for implementing the
       [Interoperability Blueprint](../../blueprint/), especially through training for awarded
       pilots and capacity building for end-users.
    2. Build **governance and coordination mechanisms** to support federation of LDTs beyond
       pilots.
    3. Facilitate collaboration and replication across pilots and other initiatives — [SIMPL](https://simpl-programme.ec.europa.eu/), the
       [LDT CitiVERSE EDIC](https://ldtcitiverse-edic.eu/), the [EU LDT Marketplace](https://interoperable-europe.ec.europa.eu/collection/ldttoolbox/solution/eu-ldt-marketplace).

=== "For other stakeholders"

    1. **Get prepared to participate** in the LDT ecosystem by aligning with the
       [Interoperability Blueprint](../../blueprint/).
    2. Where possible, **contribute to standardisation and interoperability efforts**, including
       policy interoperability and the delivery and adoption of legal enablers.

## 2 · Advancing local digitalisation at scale

Many LDT services remain **siloed and non-replicable**, limiting reuse and scalability. The focus
is on ensuring pilots' LDTs are designed as reusable, modular and replicable solutions addressing
shared challenges — so other communities can **expand existing solutions rather than developing
them from scratch**.

=== "For pilots"

    1. Design advanced LDT services with the principle of **"replicability by design"** — open
       licensing, clear documentation, use cases and guidance for reuse, and a defined plan for
       how services will be maintained over time.
    2. **Reuse resources** and leverage activities provided by LDT4SSC, other pilots, the LDT
       CitiVERSE EDIC, [DS4SSCC-DEP](https://www.ds4sscc.eu/), SIMPL and the [EU LDT Toolbox](https://interoperable-europe.ec.europa.eu/collection/ldttoolbox).

=== "For LDT4SSC"

    1. **Identify shared needs** between communities and match them with existing or new
       solutions contributed to the repository of assets and services.
    2. Support **visibility of services** through the LDT Toolbox and other channels.
    3. Define **clear responsibilities for sustaining** assets and services, and for supporting
       replicators.
    4. Encourage **decentralised maintenance** by initial developers wherever feasible. In the
       short term, assume responsibility for maintaining pilot assets only where there is clear
       evidence of continued relevance.

=== "For other stakeholders"

    1. **Reuse and adapt existing solutions** instead of developing new ones from scratch.
    2. Participate in collaboration opportunities provided by LDT4SSC.
    3. Provide **feedback on usability and applicability**.

## 3 · Demonstrating business and public value

A critical barrier to sustainability is the **limited articulation and assessment of the value
LDTs generate**. Without clear value propositions and capture mechanisms across stakeholders,
long-term sustainability stays uncertain. Linking value creation with value capture underpins
economic sustainability and supports the emergence of a structured LDT market.

[:octicons-arrow-right-24: How value is created and captured](value.md)

=== "For pilots"

    1. **Promote LDT services by assessing and demonstrating tangible value propositions**
       across stakeholders — cities, providers, citizens — supporting adoption and
       replicability. Document this in deliverables and present it in peer-learning sessions and
       Stakeholder Forum meetings.
    2. **Contribute to the evolution of business and governance models** (cost-sharing, PPPs) by
       testing viable alternatives and documenting learnings.
    3. **Valorise your services** by following the validation framework — converging alignment
       with the [Interoperability Blueprint](../../blueprint/), the [ISAQ assessment](../resources_content/tech_resources.md#interoperability-self-assessment-questionnaire-isaq), [Responsible AI Label badges](https://citcomai-hub.github.io/documentation/ai_assessment/),
       [MIM8](https://mims.oascities.org/local-digital-twins), and LDT Toolbox adoption.

=== "For LDT4SSC"

    1. **Engage extended stakeholders** across the value chain to better understand their
       business needs and support pilots in addressing them.
    2. **Collaborate with other smart communities initiatives** to assess market dynamics and
       identify gaps.
    3. **Co-create with pilot representatives** a framework for assessing value creation and
       negotiating value capture across initiatives.
    4. Collaborate with [CitCom.ai](https://citcomtef.eu/), the [LDT CitiVERSE EDIC](https://ldtcitiverse-edic.eu/), [MIM8](https://mims.oascities.org/local-digital-twins) and the EU LDT Toolbox towards a
       **full-stack validation service** for LDTs.

=== "For other stakeholders"

    1. **Test and validate** LDT4SSC assets and services' fit to the market, and provide
       feedback.
    2. Support development of **market enablers** — funding, procurement, partnerships.

## 4 · Growing the communities of practice

The LDT ecosystem is inherently **multi-stakeholder**, requiring sustained collaboration across
public authorities, private sector, academia and civil society. The aim is to grow gradually from
pilot-driven ecosystems to a **mature EU-wide LDT ecosystem**, with LDT4SSC acting as facilitator
and orchestrator — enabling peer learning and co-creation, especially among stakeholders with
differing levels of maturity.

=== "For pilots"

    1. **Engage stakeholders** across public, private, academic and civil society sectors to
       identify additional use cases and collaboration opportunities, describing the potential
       for replication and scaling in your deliverables.
    2. **Define KPIs** for (a) ecosystem growth around local implementations and (b)
       collaborations fostered through the EU-wide LDT ecosystem.

=== "For LDT4SSC"

    1. Establish a clear **engagement funnel**: identifying actors → contact-building →
       fostering participation → achieving contributions to the ecosystem → replication by other
       communities.
    2. **Invest in marketing** of the LDT ecosystem and the repository through events,
       matchmaking and capacity-building activities.

=== "For other stakeholders"

    1. **Participate in communities of practice** and knowledge exchange across communities and
       smart communities initiatives.

## 5 · Strengthening capabilities across all three levels

Sustainability depends on stakeholders' ability to develop and mobilise the necessary
**technical, organisational and governance capabilities** — across
<span class="level-dot individual">individual LDTs</span>,
<span class="level-dot ecosystem">the ecosystem</span>, and
<span class="level-dot assets">LDT4SSC's shared assets and services</span>.
This covers organisational integration and
governance, technical capacity and interoperability, business and funding models, skills
development and knowledge exchange.

=== "For pilots"

    1. **Assess and monitor internal capabilities** against the 18 capabilities above.
    2. **Participate in capacity-building activities** provided by LDT4SSC where needed.

=== "For LDT4SSC"

    1. Ensure the assets and services provided — the Interoperability Blueprint,
       capacity-building activities — are **fit for purpose** to support LDT sustainability and
       growth.

=== "For other stakeholders"

    1. **Disseminate and reuse** LDT4SSC capacity-building activities in your communities.
    2. **Contribute back** by referencing additional capacity-building activities.
    3. Provide **long-term support mechanisms** — financial and institutional.
    4. **Align research, education and innovation** with capacity-building needs.
