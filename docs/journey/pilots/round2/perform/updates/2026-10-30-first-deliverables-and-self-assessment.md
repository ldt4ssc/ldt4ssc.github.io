By the end of October 2026, PERFORM had submitted its first two deliverables, PD1 and PD2, and completed its initial pilot self-assessment.

These early outputs establish a starting point for organising project data, assessing pilot readiness and tracking progress. Contributions from the municipalities and technical partners brought together information on existing systems, available datasets and requirements for developing the LDT services.

The next steps are to refine data requirements, confirm access arrangements and use the assessment findings to guide service development in Naoussa and Vila Franca de Xira.
