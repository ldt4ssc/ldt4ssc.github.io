The **NeWG+** consortium has completed its Month 2 (`M2`) foundational deliverables—the **Data Management Plan & Ethics baseline (`PD1`)** and the initial **LDT4SSC Interoperability Self-Assessment (`PD2 V1` / ISAQ)**—establishing the governance, privacy, and technical interoperability guardrails for implementation across **Madrid** and **Codogno**.

## Key outcomes established in M2

1. **Vendor-Neutral Interoperability Baseline (`NGSI-LD` & `Smart Data Models`)**:
   - Completed the initial **Interoperability Self-Assessment Questionnaire (ISAQ)** across **OASC MIMs Plus (`MIM0`–`MIM8`)**, mapping current maturity levels across both pilot sites and defining the progression path toward a final assessment score above 3.
   - Confirmed strict adherence to open, vendor-neutral standards—anchoring semantic and context exchange in **NGSI-LD (ETSI CIM)**, **Smart Data Models (OMA-SDM)**, **DCAT-AP / TM Forum** catalogues, and **ODRL** policies so municipal digital twins retain full sovereignty without proprietary lock-in.
2. **Data Minimisation, Commercial Confidentiality & GDPR Controls (`PD1`)**:
   - Defined clear separation between operational B2B order/stock handling inside **NeWG** and governed outward data-product publication into municipal data spaces and Local Digital Twins.
   - Established role-bound visibility and aggregation rules protecting commercial sensitivity among wholesale and retail operators along the **Mercamadrid → Mercado de Maravillas** pilot corridor in Madrid and producer flows in **Codogno**, alongside strict GDPR data-minimisation and retention schedules.
3. **Preparation for `PD3` (Pilot Scope & Architecture)**:
   - Initiated technical architecture alignment sessions to freeze end-to-end component interfaces, federated data-space routing, and open-source packaging ahead of the `PD3` architecture milestone.
