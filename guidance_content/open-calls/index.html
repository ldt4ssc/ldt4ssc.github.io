    Open calls — archive

    All three rounds are closed

    The third and final round closed on 13 July 2026. This page is kept as a record of what the calls asked for — useful to awarded pilots re-reading their commitments, and to anyone tracing how the pilot portfolio was assembled.

    Obligations that continue through implementation are collected on the pilot obligations page. To follow the community, join the Stakeholder Forum; to see the pilots already running, go to the Pilot Journey.

    The three work strands

    The project ran five Calls for Pilots across three work strands, aiming to establish 15–20 pilots.

    • WS1 · Technical interconnection

      Linking and scaling digital twins already in place — demonstrating interoperability, cross-domain and cross-border data sharing, and the foundations of a European federation.

      WS1 cheat sheet

    • WS2 · Creation of LDTs

      Building new LDTs around shared urban or regional challenges — cross-border traffic, air pollution — as reusable, replicable solutions.

      WS2 cheat sheet

    • WS3 · AI-based capabilities

      Adding AI-driven, value-added services to existing LDTs, and new services to the LDT Toolbox.

      WS3 cheat sheet

    Who could apply

    Consortium composition — the minimum in every strand:

    • 2+ local/regional public authorities from 2 different eligible countries;
    • 1+ additional partner — private entity, association, trusted third party, or sector representative;
    • eligible countries: EU Member States (including outermost regions and OCTs), EEA (Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein) and DEP-associated countries;
    • preference for an EU local or regional authority as Lead Partner;
    • LDT4SSC consortium members could not participate in pilot consortia.

    Open to cities, municipalities, provincial and regional authorities, syndicates and energy communities, and technology providers and AI innovators from both public and private sectors.

    What applicants submitted

    Document Format
    Application Form PDF
    Financial Form Excel (.xlsx)
    Letter(s) of Commitment PDF
    Ownership and Control Declaration (OCD) PDF
    Ethics and Data Protection Self-Assessment PDF

    Application form content covered: data governance (current → target, across political, technical, legal and organisational dimensions); project management and resource allocation; political endorsement and technical capacity per public authority; end-user engagement; quadruple-helix stakeholder involvement (3 of 4 groups); alignment with EU priorities; replicability to other EU communities; socio-economic and environmental effects with an eco-design approach; and a post-pilot sustainability strategy.

    Applicants also provided four draft diagrams per use case — technical (deployment, current and future) and functional (data lifecycle, current and future). In WS3 the functional diagram covered the end-to-end data and AI pipeline, from collection through training, validation and testing to deployment and monitoring.

    Financial rules

    Rule Value
    Maximum grant per third party (across all pilots) €500,000
    Maximum grant per consortium €1,000,000
    Co-funding from applicants ≥ 50% of total costs
    Indirect costs 7% flat rate of direct costs
    Pilot duration 12–18 months
