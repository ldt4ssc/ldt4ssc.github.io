    Start from where you are

    Start with the implementation guidance to place yourself — which LDT level you are working at, which reference architecture applies, and how the methodology, resources and Blueprint connect.

    Then follow the workflow for your work strand:

    Your situation Workflow
    Existing twins that need to talk to each other WS1 · Interconnect existing LDTs
    A twin that does not exist yet WS2 · Deploy a new LDT
    A working twin that should predict, explain or advise WS3 · Build an AI-based service

    Keep the obligations page open alongside — several steps carry binding requirements.

    As you go, post updates on what you build and learn. They appear on your pilot's page and in the updates feed.

    The obligations page groups requirements by theme — build, interoperate, follow the methodology, engage, share back, sustain — with the Rq/Rc codes and a column per work strand.

    Codes differ between strands, so read your own column. For binding wording, go to the Call for Pilots Manual.

    The two deadlines most often missed:

    • the ISAQ in months 1–2 and again in the second reporting period — a final score above three is a condition for payment;
    • packaging and publishing assets, which is far cheaper done continuously than at the end.

    The calls are closed, but the full reference is archived: open calls for consortium conditions, financial rules and timelines, and the per-strand cheat sheets for the detailed breakdowns — WS1 · WS2 · WS3.

    The three work strands

    Each strand targets a different level of the LDT stack. They stack on one another — a WS3 service still needs the Core a WS2 pilot builds.

    WS1 · Technical interconnection of LDTs

    Linking and scaling digital twins already in place. Works at the Base and Core levels: exposing what exists through standard interfaces and connecting it to other twins.

    Architecture · Workflow · Call requirements

    WS2 · Creation of LDTs based on common needs

    Addressing shared urban or regional challenges. Builds the Core: shared data acquisition, context management and analytics, specified once and instantiated locally.

    Architecture · Workflow · Call requirements

    WS3 · Advanced AI-based capabilities

    Integrating AI-driven services into existing LDTs. Works at the Surface: AI services, simulation and immersive interfaces consuming an existing LDT and publishing results back into it.

    Architecture · Workflow · Call requirements
