On 23 September 2026, PERFORM held its online kick-off meeting, bringing together 18 participants from all consortium partners. The meeting opened with a presentation by Spiros Vlachos from CLOCKWORK, the project coordinator, followed by partner introductions.

Over 18 months, PERFORM will develop Local Digital Twin services in Naoussa, Greece, and Vila Franca de Xira, Portugal, covering eight pilot buildings, including schools, swimming pools, municipal facilities and social housing. By combining building data, energy models and AI-based analysis, the project will help municipal teams understand energy use, identify opportunities to adjust consumption and assess renewable-energy investments. The project’s two planned services will support real-time energy monitoring and flexibility management, alongside the assessment of renewable-energy investment scenarios.

Partners discussed coordination arrangements, regular project meetings and the clarification of deliverable responsibilities. We also prepared for the first in-person meeting in Naoussa, which would provide an opportunity to better understand the local pilot context and align technical work with municipal needs.
