On 19–20 October 2026, PERFORM partners met in Naoussa, Greece, to review the pilot scope, align technical activities and prepare the first project deliverables.

Both Naoussa and Vila Franca de Xira confirmed four pilot buildings, with priorities including solar self-consumption, real-time energy monitoring and renewable-energy investment assessment. Discussions clarified the two service areas: energy monitoring and flexibility support, and financial modelling to assess the viability of renewable-energy investments.

Partners reviewed data availability, interoperability requirements and the approach to retaining raw data locally within each municipality. We also discussed the data-management plan and artefact inventory, agreeing how municipal and technical partners would contribute information on datasets, systems and project outputs.

The meeting concluded with agreed responsibilities for completing the initial pilot self-assessments and deliverables, providing a clearer basis for the next stages of service development.
