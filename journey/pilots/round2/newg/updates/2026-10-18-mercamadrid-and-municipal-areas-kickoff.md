We held the **NeWG+ Madrid Operational Kick-Off** bringing together **Mercamadrid**, wholesale and retail sector associations (**AEMPM**, **ADEPESCA**, **COMAPES**), and the five participating municipal areas of **Ayuntamiento de Madrid** (*Oficina Digital / DGOD, Comercio, Movilidad, Madrid Salud, and Medio Ambiente y Energía*) to align on the 18-month execution along the **Mercamadrid → Mercado de Maravillas** pilot corridor.

## Phased rollout: Focusing deep end-to-end validation on Mercado de Maravillas

During the session, the consortium and municipal stakeholders aligned on concentrating the Madrid pilot execution on the **Mercamadrid → Mercado de Maravillas** corridor—Europe's largest municipal market and a priority **Smart Urban Space (EUI)** node—as the high-density operational benchmark and replicable blueprint for Madrid's broader municipal market network:

- **Phase F1 (M1–M4 · Gate H1)**: Activation of the initial **Mercamadrid → Mercado de Maravillas** B2B corridor with a first cohort of wholesalers and retail stalls, alongside minimum EUI/corridor service-anchor instrumentation and KPI baseline capture.
- **Phase F2 (M5–M7 · Gate H2)**: Full B2B service operation and consolidation at **Mercado de Maravillas**, calibrating end-to-end batch traceability, Digital Product Passport (DPP) views, and data-space governance policies.
- **Phase F3 (M8–M10 · Gate H3)**: Activation of B2B2C last-mile capillarity around **Mercado de Maravillas**, calibrating the multi-resolution **H3 Smart Mesh** (DUM 360 curb saturation, surrounding traffic friction, and Green IT selective ingestion).
- **Phase F4 (M11–M18 · Gate H4)**: Full operational integration of the **Mercamadrid–Maravillas** corridor with Madrid's **Twin-of-Twins (*Gemelo de Gemelos*)**, **City OS**, and **Smart Urban Spaces (EUIs)**, consolidating the open replication package for future municipal markets.

## Alignment across the 7-step operational flow and municipal areas

We reviewed the end-to-end 7-step operational flow—from multichannel order capture (**CoCo AI** on WhatsApp) and assisted warehouse preparation (**Stocker WMS** + edge IoT/RFID) to shared multi-species refrigerated transport, **H3 Smart Mesh** context enrichment, governed publication via **MIRA / InterFlux**, and LDT simulation—confirming the value return for each municipal domain:

- **Commerce (*Dirección General de Comercio*)**: Restoring logistics convenience and data sovereignty to **Mercamadrid** wholesalers and **Mercado de Maravillas** retailers without forcing digital complexity or platform lock-in.
- **Mobility (*Área de Movilidad y Transportes*)**: Consolidating multi-supplier loads (proven 5:1 vehicle reduction ratio in the 2020–2021 baseline), integrating with **DUM 360** loading bay reservations around **Mercado de Maravillas**, and simulating street restrictions before implementation.
- **Public Health (*Madrid Salud*)**: Validating multi-species refrigerated compartmentalisation (fish, meat, produce) and providing instant, role-authorized access to auditable Digital Product Passport (DPP) cold-chain and hygiene evidence.
- **Environment & Energy (*Medio Ambiente y Energía*)**: Measuring before/after impact (kWh per delivery, refrigeration/charging energy profiles, and avoided emissions) across the **Mercamadrid → Mercado de Maravillas** corridor and local delivery catchment area.
- **Digital Office (*DGOD*)**: Operationalizing **MIRA (*Minimum Interoperability Reference Architecture*)** to bridge live market operations at **Mercamadrid** and **Mercado de Maravillas** with the Mercamadrid/Mobilities4EU data space, EDINT, and the municipal Twin-of-Twins.

## Immediate 30/60/90-day actions

The operational working groups are now executing the 30/60/90-day onboarding sequence with **AEMPM**, **ADEPESCA**, and **COMAPES** to capture the baseline KPIs and complete the first **Mercamadrid–Maravillas** live workflow demonstrations ahead of **Gate H1**.
