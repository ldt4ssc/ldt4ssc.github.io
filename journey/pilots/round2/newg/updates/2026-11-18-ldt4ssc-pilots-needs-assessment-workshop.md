Representatives of the **NeWG+** consortium participated in the **LDT4SSC Needs Assessment Workshop for Pilots** organised across Work Strands 1, 2, and 3 to benchmark technical and non-technical building-block readiness and shape upcoming peer-learning activities across the European pilot cohort.

## Readiness self-assessment & architectural contribution

Within the **Work Strand 1 (Interconnecting Existing Local Digital Twins)** breakout track, **NeWG+** contributed its initial consortium readiness assessment and architectural approach:

- **Strong technical & use-case foundation**: High readiness across business-model definition, operational use-case scoping (*Mercamadrid → Mercado de Maravillas* in Madrid and the *Codogno Agrifood Corridor* in Lombardy), data sovereignty, and federated trust frameworks.
- **Federated-of-Federations & Zero-Friction Onboarding (`InterFlux SDK`)**: Shared our approach to keeping operational payloads sovereign while federating metadata catalogues across municipal data spaces, using **Capillar IT's InterFlux SDK** alongside **FIWARE / EDC** components (**SEAMWARE**) to provide a lightweight, consent-style onboarding flow for low-digital-maturity market operators.
- **Vendor neutrality by design**: Reinforced our commitment to **NGSI-LD** and **Smart Data Models** to meet public-sector open-standards policies and ensure clean portability toward the **EU LDT Toolbox Marketplace**.

## Ecosystem priorities shared with the LDT4SSC community

To accelerate delivery across WS1 pilots, we highlighted three high-value community resources for upcoming hands-on workshops and peer exchanges:

1. **Standardised transformation tooling** and reference pipelines for mapping live operational application schemas into **Smart Data Models / NGSI-LD**.
2. **Reusable legal and contractual templates** for multi-party public-private **Data Cooperation Agreements** (complementing the LDT4SSC *Data Cooperation Canvas*).
3. **Worked end-to-end reference implementations** demonstrating blueprint building blocks in live cross-city federations.
