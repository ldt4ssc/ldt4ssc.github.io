The **NeWG+** pilot (*New Wholesale Market Generation: cities digital infrastructure implementation through sustainable logistics and commerce*) has officially entered into force following the signature of the **LDT4SSC Pilot Grant Agreement** (Project 101226211 — Digital Europe Programme) for an 18-month implementation period (`2026-08-31` to `2028-02-29`).

## What we are building

Under **Work Strand 1 (Interconnecting Existing Local Digital Twins)**, NeWG+ interconnects two operational Local Digital Twins across complementary urban and territorial morphologies without forcing symmetrical technical stacks or centralized cross-border data lakes:

1. **Madrid (*Market-to-City* Flagship)**: Led by **Ayuntamiento de Madrid — Dirección General de la Oficina Digital (DGOD)** with **Mercamadrid** (Europe's largest fresh-food wholesale hub), **COMAPES / AEMPM / ADEPESCA**, and **Mercado de Maravillas** (the pilot municipal market anchor and Smart Urban Space / EUI reference node for Madrid's municipal market network). Low-digital-maturity wholesalers, logistics operators, and B2B buyers adopt **NeWG** via an *onboarding-by-service* pattern (CoCo AI order capture over WhatsApp, Stocker warehouse preparation, shared multi-temperature dispatch, and Digital Product Passport continuity), generating governed data products via **MIRA & InterFlux** (with **SEAMWARE** FIWARE/EDC data-space components) and **H3 Smart Mesh** context enrichment for Madrid's **Twin-of-Twins (*Gemelo de Gemelos*)**, **City OS**, and **Smart Urban Spaces (EUIs)**.
2. **Codogno (*Territory-to-City* Agrifood Corridor Twin)**: Led by **Comune di Codogno** and **Politecnico di Milano (POLIMI)** in Lombardy's dairy and agrifood basin, extending Codogno's operational 3D Local Digital Twin in CesiumJS with a lightweight agrifood-flow observatory, local H3 smart mesh, corridor modelling toward **Milano / Sogemi**, and a bounded shared-dispatch validation package.

## Consortium & Governance Structure

The six-partner consortium brings together two public administrations, two technology providers, and two European research institutions across Spain, Italy, and France:

- **Ayuntamiento de Madrid — DGOD (Spain)**: Lead Authority, Steering Committee chair, and owner of the Madrid Capital Digital / Twin-of-Twins / EUI integration route.
- **Comune di Codogno (Italy)**: Second Public Authority and owner of the Codogno Agrifood Corridor Twin and local stakeholder activation.
- **capillar IT (Spain)**: Technical Coordinator & Product Delivery Lead across both sites (NeWG operational suite, Shelter Gateway, DPP continuity, and H3 Smart Mesh).
- **SEAMWARE (Spain)**: Data-Space Technology & Governance Lead (FIWARE Data Space Connector, Eclipse Dataspace Components, TM Forum/DCAT catalogues, ODRL/ABAC usage control).
- **Mines Paris — PSL (France)**: Scientific & Validation Board Chair (Smart Mesh methodology, Green IT selective ingestion, KPI validation, and transferability).
- **Politecnico di Milano — POLIMI (Italy)**: Technical-Scientific Lead for the Codogno workstream (territorial analysis, observatory design, corridor simulation, and WS1 transfer package).

## Next steps (M1–M2: Explore Phase)

During the initial two months, the consortium is executing **Work Package 1** and **Work Package 2** startup activities:

- Establishing the **Data Management Plan & Ethics baseline (`PD1`)** and the initial **LDT4SSC Interoperability Self-Assessment (`PD2 V1`)**.
- Activating the initial Mercamadrid–Maravillas operational cohort (**Phase F1**) and mapping producer/market actors in Codogno.
