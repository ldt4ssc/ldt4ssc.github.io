The MUNICIPAL pilot project has officially launched following our kick-off meeting hosted by the City Council of Murcia on July 7th and 8th. The event brought together our consortium of seven partners from Cyprus, Greece, Denmark, and Spain to align on objectives, the implementation approach, and upcoming activities.

## What we did
During the event, partners explored Murcia's smart city ecosystem first-hand. We reviewed the MiMurcia digital twin platform and the city's Single Monitoring Centre (CEUS), discussing how municipal services currently integrate data. 

## What this enables
This kick-off sets the stage for our core engineering challenge: linking the MiMurcia and iniCOSIA local digital twin platforms via the SIMPL/CEEDS interoperability framework. By making these distinct digital twins talk to one another securely, we will combine Murcia's well-being and urban services data with Nicosia's energy and mobility IoT capabilities.

As Dr. Magda Foti, Head of the Energy Digitalization (EDG) Research Group at UBITECH, noted during the launch: *"By interconnecting MiMurcia and iniCOSIA through a shared interoperability framework, we are building the technical foundation for cities to exchange data securely and make better, faster, AI-supported decisions together."*

## Next steps
Our immediate focus is on defining the shared data models and establishing the initial technical architecture for the SIMPL components that will enable this federated, cross-border data exchange.
